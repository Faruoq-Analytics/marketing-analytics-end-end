# marketing-analytics-end-end

## Business Scenario
**Company**
**BrightMart Retail**

BrightMart is an e-commerce company that sells electronics, fashion, and home products across multiple regions.
The company spends a large amount of money every month on digital marketing.

Marketing channels include:
- Google Ads
- Facebook
- Intagram
- Email
- LinkedIn

Unfortunately, the executive team has noticed several problems.

### Business Problem Statement

Over the last twelve months, BrightMart has increased its marketing budget by more than 30%, but revenue
growth has not kept pace.

Different marketing teams maintain campaign data in separate spreadsheets, making it difficult to answer
important business questions such as:

- Which marketing campaign channel generates the highest return on investmennt (ROI)?
- Which campaigns are wasting advertising budget?
- Whcih regions respond best to digital marketing?
- Are conversions improving over time?
- Which channels should be discontinued?
- How much revenue is generated for every dollar spent?
- What trends should executives know before planning next quarter's budget?

Because there is no centralized reporting sysytem, management spends days manually combining Excel reports before
every monthly meeting. This delays decision-making and increases the risk of reporting errors.

The leadership team has therefore requested a centralized marketing analytics solution that stores campaign data in a
structured database, enables reliale reporting through SQL, and delivers an interactive Power BI dashboard for ongoing
performance monitoring.

#### Project Objective
Our objective is to build an end-to-end Marketing Analytics solution that:

1. Designs a relational database for marketing campaign data.
2. Create SQL tables with appropriate contraints.
3. Inserts realistic sample data.
4. Cleans and validates the data.
5. Models relationships where approapriate.
6. Performs exploaratory analysis.
7. Answers key business questions using SQL
8. Calculate marketing KPIs such as:
   - Click-Through Rate (CTR)
   - Conversion Rate
   - Cost per Click (CPC)
   - Cost per Acquisition (CPA)
   - Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
   - Return on investment (ROI)
9. Creates SQL views for reporting.
10. Builds a power BI dashbord with interactive visuals
11. Presents executive recommendations based on the findings.
